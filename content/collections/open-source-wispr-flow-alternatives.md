## What Wispr Flow does, and what an alternative has to match

Wispr Flow is a subscription dictation app: hold a key, speak, and it transcribes in the cloud and rewrites what you said into clean text in whatever app you are using. An open-source alternative has to match the hotkey-to-cursor loop; the better ones also match the cleanup. The real difference between them is where your audio goes, and whether the ready-made build costs money.

## How to choose

| | [Handy](/apps/handy/) | [Voicetypr](/apps/voicetypr/) | [OpenLess](/apps/openless/) | [VoiceInk](/apps/voiceink/) | [FluidVoice](/apps/fluidvoice/) | [FreeFlow](/apps/freeflow/) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windows | Yes | Yes | Yes | — | — | — |
| macOS | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Linux | Yes | — | Yes | — | — | — |
| Transcription by default | Local | Local | Cloud | Local | Local | Cloud (Groq) |
| AI cleanup | Optional | Optional | Core | Yes | Optional | Core |
| Builds | Free | Paid after trial | Free | Paid after trial | Free | Free |
| Licence | MIT | AGPL-3.0 | AGPL-3.0 | GPL-3.0 | GPL-3.0 | MIT |

- **On Windows and want it offline and free?** Handy.
- **On Windows and want AI formatting in a polished app?** Voicetypr.
- **Want speech rewritten the way Wispr does it?** OpenLess, or FreeFlow on a Mac.
- **On a Mac and privacy comes first?** VoiceInk or FluidVoice.

[Voicebox](/apps/voicebox/) is here too for people who want dictation and voice cloning in one app.

- **Want meeting transcripts from the same app?** OpenWhispr.
- **Keep fighting misspelt names and jargon?** Voquill's glossary.
- **Want tone to follow the app you are in?** Amical.

## Not listed and why

- **SayIt**, a Windows-only voice-typing app, is promising but under 500 stars and a few months old; it joins after a few more releases.
- **Projects named LocalFlow** are macOS-only with a few dozen stars at most, and none imports Wispr Flow history.
- **Apps with a few stars and no release** join when they ship an installable build.

For speech output rather than input, see [open-source ElevenLabs alternatives](/collections/open-source-elevenlabs-alternatives/). More desktop apps are under [Productivity](/categories/productivity/), and several of these are [Tauri](/stacks/tauri/) apps.
