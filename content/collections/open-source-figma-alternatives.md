## What Figma does, and what an alternative has to match

Figma is a hosted, browser-based design tool: teams design interfaces together in real time, build design systems from components and variables, prototype flows, and hand off to developers through Dev Mode. An open-source alternative has to match that shared canvas, the design-system tooling and the handoff — and, for many teams, let them keep design files on their own servers.

## How to choose

| | [Penpot](/apps/penpot/) | [OpenDesign](/apps/open-design/) |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Design and prototype by hand, together | AI agents generate the design |
| Design systems | Components, variants, design tokens | A `DESIGN.md` brand file |
| Output | Designs, prototypes, CSS/SVG/HTML inspect | HTML prototypes, PDF, PPTX, images, MP4 |
| Self-host | Docker, Kubernetes | Local-first desktop and web app |
| Licence | MPL-2.0 | Apache-2.0 |
| Since | 2015 | April 2026 |

- **Replacing Figma for a design team?** Penpot.
- **Want prototypes and decks from a prompt, with your own model?** OpenDesign.

## Not listed and why
