## What Retool does, and what an alternative has to match

Retool is a commercial platform for building internal tools: drag components onto a canvas, connect them to databases and APIs, add code where the visual builder runs out, and share the result with your team under access controls. An open-source alternative has to cover that loop — components, data sources, custom code, permissions and deployment — on your own infrastructure. The real differences are in which features each project keeps in its paid edition.

## How to choose

| | [Appsmith](/apps/appsmith/) | [ToolJet](/apps/tooljet/) | [Budibase](/apps/budibase/) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emphasis | Internal tools on your data | Internal tools, paid AI generation | Operations: apps, automations, agents |
| Built-in database | No | ToolJet Database | Data tables |
| Data sources | 25+ databases and any API | 90+ | Databases, AI models and business apps |
| Licence | Apache-2.0 | AGPL-3.0, enterprise submodules | GPL-3.0, BSL pro package |
| Since | 2020 | 2021 | 2019 |

- **Want the most permissive licence on the whole platform?** Appsmith.
- **Need a database inside the builder, or Python in apps?** ToolJet.
- **Automating requests and approvals for many users?** Budibase.

## Not listed and why

- **NocoBase** is a no-code platform for business systems, but its licence agreement adds terms to Apache-2.0 — including a ban on offering it as a hosted no-code, low-code or AI platform — so it is not open source.
- **Smaller internal-tool builders** join when they reach a stable release and steady development.

To keep the data itself in a spreadsheet-style database, see [open-source Airtable alternatives](/collections/open-source-airtable-alternatives/). More builders are under [Developer Tools](/categories/developer-tools/).
