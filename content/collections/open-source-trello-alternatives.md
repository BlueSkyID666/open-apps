## What Trello does, and what an alternative has to match

Trello is Atlassian's hosted Kanban service: boards of lists and cards that a team drags across columns, with checklists, due dates, attachments and add-ons called Power-Ups. An open-source alternative has to match that simple board, keep it in sync for everyone looking at it, and let you bring your existing boards across — on a server you control.

## How to choose

| | [WeKan](/apps/wekan/) | [Vikunja](/apps/vikunja/) | [Kanboard](/apps/kanboard/) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views | Kanban with swimlanes | List, Gantt, table, Kanban | Kanban board |
| Trello import | Yes | Yes | Not listed |
| Stack | Meteor, MongoDB | Go and Vue | PHP |
| Development | Several releases a week | Regular releases | Maintenance mode |
| Licence | MIT | AGPL-3.0 | MIT |

- **Want Trello's boards on your own server?** WeKan.
- **Want to-dos, reminders and a Gantt chart too?** Vikunja.
- **Want something tiny that just works?** Kanboard.

## Not listed and why

- **Focalboard** describes itself as an alternative to Trello, Notion and Asana, but its repository has had no commits since June 2025.
- **Leantime** calls itself "as simple as Trello but as feature-rich as Jira"; it is a full project manager, compared in [open-source Jira alternatives](/collections/open-source-jira-alternatives/).

For a whiteboard rather than a board, see [open-source Miro alternatives](/collections/open-source-miro-alternatives/). More self-hosted software is under [Productivity](/categories/productivity/).
