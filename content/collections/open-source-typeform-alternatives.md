## What Typeform does, and what an alternative has to match

Typeform is a hosted form and survey builder known for its conversational style: one question at a time, with logic that adapts to answers, custom branding, and integrations that send responses to other tools. An open-source alternative has to match that loop — build, share or embed, collect, analyse — on your own servers, and ideally keep response data out of third-party hands. The projects below take different parts of that job.

## How to choose

| | [HeyForm](/apps/heyform/) | [Formbricks](/apps/formbricks/) | [OpnForm](/apps/opnform/) | [LimeSurvey](/apps/limesurvey/) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | Conversational forms | In-app and web surveys | Everyday web forms | Research surveys |
| Logic | Conditional logic, redirects | Targeting by user group | Form logic | Skip logic and branching |
| Stack | Node and React | Next.js | Laravel and Nuxt | PHP |
| Licence | AGPL-3.0 | AGPL-3.0 core, enterprise directory | AGPL-3.0 core, enterprise directory | GPL-2.0 or later |
| Since | 2024 (current repository) | 2022 | 2022 | 2006 |

- **Want forms that feel like Typeform?** HeyForm.
- **Collecting feedback inside your product?** Formbricks.
- **Need contact, signup and order forms on many sites?** OpnForm.
- **Running academic or multilingual questionnaires?** LimeSurvey.

## Not listed and why

- **Typebot** builds chat-style forms and bots, but its licence is the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1, converting to Apache-2.0 later), which is source-available, not open source.
- **Form builders with a few dozen stars** join when they have a stable release and a track record.
