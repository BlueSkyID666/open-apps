## What Qualtrics does, and what an alternative has to match

Qualtrics is an enterprise survey and experience-management platform: questionnaires with complex logic, distribution by link, email and in-product, and analysis of the results. An open-source alternative has to cover the survey itself, its distribution and its results — on a server you control.

## How to choose

| | [Formbricks](/apps/formbricks/) | [LimeSurvey](/apps/limesurvey/) |
|---|---|---|
| Best at | In-app, website and customer surveys | Research questionnaires |
| Distribution | Link, website, in-app, email | Link, invitations |
| Stack | Docker | PHP, web server, database |
| Licence | AGPL core, paid enterprise directory | GPL-2.0 |

- **Feedback inside your product?** Formbricks.
- **Academic or market research?** LimeSurvey.

## Not listed and why

Form builders focused on single forms rather than research surveys are in [open-source Typeform alternatives](/collections/open-source-typeform-alternatives/). More business software is under [Business](/categories/business/).
