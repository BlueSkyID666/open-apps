## What Otter.ai does, and what an alternative has to match

Otter.ai is a hosted meeting assistant: it transcribes meetings live with speaker recognition, turns them into summaries with decisions and action items, and makes every conversation searchable — with lectures and interviews as well as calls. An open-source alternative has to produce an accurate, searchable transcript; the better ones also label speakers and write a summary. The difference is where the audio goes. Every app below transcribes on your own computer.

## How to choose

| | [OpenWhispr](/apps/openwhispr/) | [Minutes](/apps/minutes/) | [Meetily](/apps/meetily/) | [Buzz](/apps/buzz/) | [Vibe](/apps/vibe/) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main job | Dictation and meetings | Meetings and voice memos | Meetings | Recordings and live mic | Recordings and system audio |
| Meeting detection | Zoom, Teams, FaceTime | "Call detected" banner (macOS) | PRO edition only | Not a meeting app | Not a meeting app |
| Speaker labels | Yes | Yes | Planned for PRO | Yes | Yes |
| Summaries | Local or cloud model | Your chosen provider | Local, through Ollama | Through a plugin | Ollama or Claude API |
| Platforms | macOS, Windows, Linux | macOS, Windows, Linux CLI | macOS, Windows | macOS, Windows, Linux | macOS, Windows, Linux |
| Licence | MIT | MIT | MIT | MIT | MIT |

- **Want Otter's whole meeting loop on your own machine?** OpenWhispr.
- **Want your coding agent to remember your meetings?** Minutes.
- **Confidential meetings, nothing may leave the laptop?** Meetily, with a local model your hardware can run.
- **Have a folder of interviews or lectures to transcribe?** Buzz or Vibe.

## Not listed and why

- **Browser-only and hosted transcription services** are not here: an alternative has to run on your own hardware or a server you control.
- **Dictation-only apps** such as [Handy](/apps/handy/) type what you say but do not transcribe meetings or files; they are in [open-source Wispr Flow alternatives](/collections/open-source-wispr-flow-alternatives/).
- **Smaller meeting recorders** with a few dozen stars or no release join when they ship an installable build.

For notetakers modelled on Granola's notepad, see [open-source Granola alternatives](/collections/open-source-granola-alternatives/). More apps like these are under [Productivity](/categories/productivity/) and [Media](/categories/media/).
