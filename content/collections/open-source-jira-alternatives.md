## What Jira does, and what an alternative has to match

Jira is Atlassian's work-tracking platform: issues and custom workflows, Scrum and Kanban boards, backlogs, roadmaps and reports, sold as a cloud service and, for self-hosting, as Jira Data Center. An open-source alternative has to cover the planning loop — issues, boards, sprints and reports — on your own servers, and ideally be easier to run than Jira itself. The four below differ mostly in scope: from portfolio management down to a clean agile board.

## How to choose

| | [OpenProject](/apps/openproject/) | [Plane](/apps/plane/) | [Leantime](/apps/leantime/) | [Taiga](/apps/taiga/) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | Portfolio, agile and classic PM | Software issue tracking | Goals and simple planning | Scrum and Kanban |
| Stack | Ruby on Rails | Django, React | PHP | Django, AngularJS front end |
| Paid extras | Enterprise add-ons | Commercial Edition | Enterprise plugins | Hosted service |
| Licence | GPL-3.0 | AGPL-3.0 CE | AGPL-3.0 | MPL-2.0 and AGPL-3.0 |
| Since | 2012 | 2022 | 2015 | 2021 (current repositories) |

- **Replacing Jira and MS Project across an organisation?** OpenProject.
- **A software team that wants something like Linear?** Plane.
- **Most of the team are not project managers?** Leantime.
- **Sprints and boards, nothing more?** Taiga.

## Not listed and why

- **Focalboard** describes itself as an alternative to Trello, Notion and Asana, but its repository has had no commits since June 2025.
- **Lighter Kanban boards** such as [WeKan](/apps/wekan/) and [Kanboard](/apps/kanboard/) are closer to Trello than to Jira; they are compared in [open-source Trello alternatives](/collections/open-source-trello-alternatives/).

For trackers built for software teams in the Linear style, see [open-source Linear alternatives](/collections/open-source-linear-alternatives/). More team software is under [Productivity](/categories/productivity/).
